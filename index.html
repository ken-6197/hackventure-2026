HackVenture 2026

HackVenture 2026 is a technical hackathon jointly organized by NIT Mizoram and the Mizoram Police Department, bringing together students, developers, and technology enthusiasts to build innovative solutions for real-world challenges in policing and public safety.

The event encourages participants to apply technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Data Analytics, Web Development, and emerging digital technologies to develop practical and impactful solutions.

HackVenture builds on the collaboration between NIT Mizoram and Mizoram Police seen in earlier HackVenture editions, including the 2023 event focused on technology-driven solutions for challenges faced by police personnel.

Problem Statements

Develop practical digital solutions for policing and public safety in Mizoram.

01 Automated Check Gate Monitoring System Vehicle records, verification, alerts, and movement analytics +

Design a data-capture tool and analytics dashboard that can:

  1. Automate or facilitate digital records of vehicles entering and exiting check gates using ANPR or another suitable method.
  2. Acquire or verify vehicle registration details against the VAHAN database.
  3. Acquire or verify driver details against the SARTHI database.
  4. Generate real-time alerts for suspicious vehicles entering or exiting, including stolen vehicles and vehicles previously involved in crimes such as drug trafficking or hit-and-run cases.
  5. Record action-taken reports when suspicious vehicles are intercepted.
  6. Generate analytics reports on the nature and patterns of vehicle movement at check gates.

Operational priority: Capture information as quickly as possible to avoid vehicle congestion at check gates.

Contact: Pu Shivendu Bhushan, IPS, SP, Hnahthial. 8960419775

02 Chatbot for the Mizoram Police Bilingual public assistance and secure community reporting +

Develop an intelligent chatbot for police departments to improve operational efficiency, communication, and community engagement. It should give the public a secure way to share information while protecting individual anonymity and safety.

  1. Answer public inquiries, provide timely assistance, and support seamless interaction with law enforcement agencies.
  2. Support both Mizo and English, with Mizo as the default language and an option to switch to English.
  3. Provide password-protected access for authorized users at multiple levels, such as district, range, and state, to monitor responses and performance.
  4. Show an alert to pre-designated personnel when an individual submits sensitive information, enabling timely action.

These are broad requirements; the application should support future add-ons and evolving needs.

The primary objective is a scalable, accessible, and user-friendly digital solution that fosters trust, transparency, and collaboration between police departments and the community.

Contact: Pu Manjeet Singh, IPS, Dy. SP (Hqrs), Lunglei. 9462006850

03 Social Media Monitoring (SMM) Tool Bilingual search, analysis, and crisis detection +

Social media has become a major hub for cybercrime, including cyber fraud, social engineering, cyberbullying, cyberattacks, and cyberterrorism. Develop an SMM tool to help law enforcement agencies monitor these platforms as part of cyber defense. Developers may add further capabilities.

The tool should include keyword-based search and analysis, with the ability to:

  • Search with Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) and phrase search.
  • Filter, clean up, group, mark, and delete collected mentions, including filtering or grouping by geography and language.
  • Analyze volume, engagement, reach, sentiment, and influence of social media mentions.
  • Detect crisis situations using sentiment analysis and daily mention monitoring.
  • Measure social media quantitatively by counting specific content, and qualitatively by assessing the opinions and beliefs expressed in content.
  • Support both English and Mizo.

Contact: Pu Gavit Gogna, IPS, Dy. SP (Crime). 9013224506

04 App for Police Crime Report Paperless daily reporting with hierarchical access and analysis +

Police stations currently submit two daily reports manually to Police Headquarters for compilation and review by the DGP: the Police Station Daily Report and the Arrested Persons Report. Develop a mobile and web-based application to make this reporting process entirely paperless.

  • Allow reports to be entered or uploaded at Police Station level.
  • Flag compliance with the stipulated upload deadline, such as 8:00 am.
  • Support five user roles with jurisdiction-based visibility:
    • PS: Submit reports for the Police Station.
    • SDPO: View reports from all Police Stations under their supervision.
    • SP: View reports from all SDPOs under their supervision.
    • DIG: View reports from all SPs within their jurisdiction.
    • DGP-IGP: View reports from all Police Stations.
  • Generate daily, weekly, and monthly reports, with PDF export.
  • Provide search and reporting based on selected fields to support analysis of Police Station data.

Sample reports are provided in Annexures I and II.

Contact: Pu Zonun Sanga, Addl. SP, CID Crime. 8415846135

Guidelines & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about eligibility, stages, rules, and code submissions.

Who is eligible to participate in HackVenture: Hack 'N' Solve? +

Any undergraduate or postgraduate student enrolled in recognized universities or colleges globally is eligible. Inter-college and cross-specialization teams (e.g. engineering + design) are actively encouraged.

What is the team size and composition? +

Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members. You can compete solo or use our built-in Team Matcher tool above to find co-builders before the registration deadline.

How does the hybrid format work? +

Stage 1 (Ideation) and Stage 2 (48-Hour Virtual Hackathon) are conducted 100% online so anyone can participate remotely. The top 15 shortlisted teams from Stage 2 advance to Stage 3 for the Grand Offline Finale hosted at the campus auditorium with travel/stay provisions.

Is there any registration fee? +

No, registration is 100% free of cost for all participants. Free sponsor API access, compute credits, and mentor office hours are provided to all verified teams.

Who owns the intellectual property (IP)? +

You and your team retain 100% ownership of your code, design assets, and intellectual property. The organizers and sponsors claim zero rights over your creations.
